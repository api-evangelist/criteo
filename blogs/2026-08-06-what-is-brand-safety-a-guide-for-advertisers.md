Every ad placement shapes how consumers perceive your brand. When ads appear alongside harmful, misleading, or inappropriate content, the impact can erode consumer trust and damage brand reputation. As digital advertising becomes more complex, brand safety has evolved from a campaign setting into a core marketing priority.
